## Assumptions
- We already have everthing required to make the peanut butter sandwich. This includes two slice of bread on a plate and a open jar of peanut butter with a butter knife in it. 

## Steps
- Grab the butter knife by the handle with your right hand. 
- Grab the jar of peanut butter with your right hand so you dont knock the jar over when pulling the butter knife out.
- With your right hand gentle pull the peaunt butter knife out of the jar.
- Then let go of the peanut butter jar with your left hand and pick up a slice of bread.
- Make sure the pieces of bread is laying flat on top of your balm.
- Take the end of the butter knife with the peanut butter on it and lay it on the upward facing surface of the bread.
- Gentle scrape the butter knife across of the surface of the bread covering the whole surface of the slice with peanut butter.
- Then put the butter knife back into the jar.
- Then with you now empty right hand grab the other slice of bread.
- Lay the slice of bread in your right hand on top of peanut butter coverd surface of the slice in your left hand.

## Done When
- We have to slices of bread laying on top of each other with peanut butter between them.
